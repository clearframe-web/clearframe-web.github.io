Questions

Good to know.

How long does a website take?

Starter sites take about a week, Business sites about two weeks, and Pro sites three to four weeks. It depends on how quickly we get your photos and text.

Do I need to provide anything?

Your logo (if you have one), photos, prices and a few lines about your business. If you don't have text ready, we'll help you write it.

How do I pay?

50% when we start and 50% when you're happy with the finished site, before it goes live.

Can I change my website later?

Yes. Small updates after launch are quick and affordable, and Pro sites let you edit content yourself.
